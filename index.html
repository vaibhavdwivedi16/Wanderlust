Travel-Related Information

Visa & Documentation

Ensure your passport is valid for at least 6 months past your travel dates. Check entry visa requirements well in advance.

Currency & Payments

Carry a mix of local currency in cash for small vendors and international credit cards with zero foreign transaction fees.

Local Etiquette

Research local customs, basic greeting phrases, and proper dress codes for religious sites to travel respectfully.
